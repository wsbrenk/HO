# Privacy Policy

The developer team of the Hattrick Organizer (HO) takes your privacy seriously.

## Operation

After installing the HO application on your own computer, no data is transmitted to remote services during operation,
except to Hattrick via the designated CHPP API. See [Hattrick's privacy policy](https://www88.hattrick.org/Help/Privacy.aspx?view=Privacy
).

Protecting the local data is up to the user.

## Github's privacy policy

Regarding access to our project pages on GitHub, we refer you to the [GitHub Privacy Policy](
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Third Party Tools

The user is responsible for transferring data from the HO to other third-party tools, which may potentially contain personal information.
There is no automatic transfer of such data to other third-party tools.

## Changes To This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or change our Privacy Policy at any time and you should check this Privacy Policy periodically. we make any material changes to this Privacy Policy,we will notify you either through the email address you have provided us or by placing a prominent notice on our app
